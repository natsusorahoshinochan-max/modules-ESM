# Decide which defensive checks to retain, narrow, or remove

Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08

## Question

For every defensive behavior in the review inventory, is it a required safety
invariant, a correctly placed but over-broad policy, duplicated enforcement, or
speculative rejection with no contract basis? Decide the proper layer for
secret redaction, Environment Configuration classification, self-pair and
constraint policies, source-tree integrity, forbidden-evidence substitutes,
storage no-follow/atomicity, and bounded but diagnosable retained failures.
